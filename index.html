The Book

What's Inside

This isn't a fitness manual. It's a raw, honest account of what happens when you commit to showing up — every day — no matter what.

The Challenge

100 days. A 5k minimum every single day. Rain, shine, exhaustion, injury — the rule never changes. Follow the journey from day one doubt to day one hundred triumph.

The Mindset Shift

Discover how daily commitment rewires your identity. Stop being someone who "tries to be healthy" and start becoming someone who simply is.

The Transformation

Honest, unfiltered real-world results — physical, mental, and emotional. What really happens to your body and mind when you refuse to quit.

Your Blueprint

Lessons you can apply starting today. Build the relentless streak habit that changes everything — no gym membership, no special equipment required.

"You don't need to be fast. You don't need to be fit. You just need to show up — today, and then tomorrow, and then the day after that."

The Author

Louis CoffeeMeyer

Louis CoffeeMeyer isn't a professional athlete or a lifelong runner. He's someone who decided enough was enough — and then proved, one kilometre at a time, that transformation is available to anyone willing to be relentless about it.

His 100-day running streak became more than a fitness challenge. It became a philosophy for life: show up, do the work, and let consistency do the rest. Louis shares that journey with honesty, humour, and hard-won wisdom in Fat2Fit.
